Tiancheng (Alex) Zhao

My research focuses on two areas.

  • Efficient Data Structures and Parallel Graph Algorithms. I design cache-efficient data structures for compact string dictionaries and develop density-aware parallel algorithms for graph spectral sparsification.
  • Rendering and Robotics Simulation. I explore hardware–software co-design for 3D Gaussian rendering and use Incremental Potential Contact (IPC) for robust contact simulation in robotics.

I am grateful to my mentors and collaborators for their guidance and support. They have set an example for me as a researcher —— always doing interesting things, pursuing own interests and sticking to them.
